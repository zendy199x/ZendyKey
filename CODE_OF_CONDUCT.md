# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Trolling, insulting comments, personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing private information without consent
- Other unprofessional conduct

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project team. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
